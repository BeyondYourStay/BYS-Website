From hidden calm to a full calendar: Texel beyond the horizon

Boutique Hotel Texel
The challenge

Martine and the team had built a farm-to-table hotel around quiet: unhurried mornings, garden-grown ingredients, a spa made for stillness. That calm was exactly what made it hard to translate online, most hotel content on social media shouts, and shouting would have broken the spell.

What we did

We circled the property slowly before posting anything, spending time in the restaurant, the spa and the rooms to find the pace the brand actually moved at. From there we built a content rhythm that mirrored the hotel itself: fewer, slower posts, built around light, texture and the small rituals of a stay, rather than promotions.

The result

The result reads as an extension of the hotel rather than an advertisement for it. Engagement grew steadily rather than spiking, and Martine now has a visual language her team can keep using long after a shoot day ends.

Content

"They beautifully translated the atmosphere, experience and quality we offer our guests into powerful and inviting visuals."

Martine, Boutique Hotel Texel

From a feeling to a rhythm: Bed & Bos finds its wingbeat

Boutique Hotel Bed & Bos
The challenge

Annemarie could feel exactly what her hotel stood for, the quiet in the woods, the informal warmth of a place run by someone who genuinely loves it, but she couldn't name it from the outside, and her social channels felt scattered because of it.

What we did

We started by listening rather than shooting: long conversations with Annemarie about why she opened the hotel and what she wanted guests to feel. From there we set a steady wingbeat instead of scattered posts, a consistent rhythm and visual identity built around the woods, the rooms and her own voice.

The result

The connection was immediate, and the impact came faster than expected: within the first few months the Instagram channel grew to 19,000 views and Facebook to 13,000, and Bed & Bos now has a channel that finally sounds like Annemarie, with a following that grows through word of mouth as much as through the feed itself.

Content

"From the very first phone call, there was an immediate connection, Yoran and Sidney deliver outstanding work."

Annemarie van Genugten, Boutique Hotel Bed & Bos

Still in flight: Bloemenhof finds its voice

Het Bloemenhof
The challenge

Het Bloemenhof has real character, worn wooden beams, a garden that feels lived-in, a history that shows, but nobody had ever circled the place long enough to put that character into words or images. The hotel's own team saw it every day and had stopped noticing it.

What we did

We're spending time on-site across seasons instead of a single shoot day, mapping the details a returning guest would recognise: the creak of a particular staircase, the light in the breakfast room, the way the garden changes. That material is becoming the tone and rhythm of the hotel's channels.

The result (so far)

This one is still in flight, we're finding the voice as we go, together with the team, so the hotel finally sounds and looks the way it feels to actually stay there.

From a quiet view to thousands of new eyes: Dushi takes flight

Dushi Sea View
The challenge

Cor's beach bungalows sit behind a view striking enough to stop anyone scrolling, but the existing content hadn't caught up to it yet, it looked like every other beach stay instead of the one with that particular horizon.

What we did

Rather than a scripted shoot, we caught one unscripted sunrise flight over the bungalows, letting the view carry the story instead of forcing a script onto it. It set the tone for everything that followed: content built around light and place first, styling second.

The result

That first video already brought many new, genuine followers, with more impact than Cor had expected from a single piece of content, and it's now the reference point for everything we make for Dushi.

"Our first video experience already brought many new, genuine followers, even more impact than we expected."

Cor Rutten, Dushi Sea View Beach Bungalow
Beach Club & Restaurant
The Hague, The Netherlands

More than a sea view: capturing the feeling of Beachhouse Kijkduin

Beachhouse Kijkduin
The challenge

Beachhouse Kijkduin already has a strong identity and an atmosphere that speaks for itself. The challenge was to capture that same feeling on camera: the movement on the terrace, the food leaving the kitchen, the coastline and the energy of a busy afternoon, without turning it into a standard showcase of the venue.

What we did

We approached the shoot around experience rather than facilities. Instead of simply showing what was there, we created different short-form formats built around movement, rhythm and real moments. Faster sequences captured the energy of the beach club, while slower shots gave space to the food, surroundings and details that shape the experience.

The result

The result is a diverse collection of content that feels distinctly Beachhouse Kijkduin. Each piece shows a different side of the experience while staying connected through the same atmosphere, creating content that doesn't just show the place, but gives people a sense of being there.
