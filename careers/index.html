Help us build the brand side of this.

We help other places attract the right people. This is how we handle our own hiring: no recruiter in between, just directly.

We're a small team. That means whoever joins doesn't disappear into an org chart somewhere, they work directly on real clients and real work.

What this is about

We're looking for someone who can build brand and visual identity for hotels, restaurants, attractions and resorts, from first conversation to finished result.

What you'll do

  • Develop brand and visual identity for new and existing clients
  • Build logos, color and typography systems, and brand guidelines
  • Translate a place's character into a visual system that holds up across every channel
  • Present your own work to clients, no one presenting it for you

What we're looking for

  • A portfolio that shows you can build a brand, not just draw a logo
  • Experience with hospitality, lifestyle or leisure brands is a plus, not a requirement
  • You're comfortable owning a project from start to finish
  • The same instinct we work from: understand first, then build

How to apply

Send your portfolio and a short note to info@beyondyourstay.com, or reach out through the contact form. No formal cover letter needed, just show us what you've made.

What this is about

Strategy is the foundation under every piece of content we make. We're looking for someone who can read a place and translate that into a strategy the content team can execute, never a template, always something that fits.

What you'll do

  • Build content strategies rooted in who the guest actually is
  • Define content pillars, tone of voice, and channel and format strategy per client
  • Translate what you see on location into a strategy that fits, not a generic playbook
  • Track performance and adjust the strategy as it runs

What we're looking for

  • Experience building social media strategy, ideally for hospitality, lifestyle or leisure brands
  • You're comfortable turning a vague sense of atmosphere into a concrete, actionable plan
  • You watch before you act: understanding a place before building a strategy around it
  • You present strategy confidently and directly to clients

How to apply

Send your portfolio or past strategy work and a short note to info@beyondyourstay.com, or reach out through the contact form.
