About Beyond Your Stay

Not a visit. A feeling.

A visit is more than a booking, a ticket, or an hour on the schedule. It's an experience, a memory, a feeling people carry with them. We help exceptional places make that feeling visible.

"Every memorable experience deserves an online presence that reflects the atmosphere and feeling people will remember in real life."

That belief is what drives Beyond Your Stay. We work only with places that have a soul: hotels, restaurants, attractions, wellness destinations and holiday parks with real character, run by people who understand their offering is an experience, not a product.

Meet the founder

"Travel has always been a big part of my life. From a young age, I felt the urge to explore new places, experience different cultures and meet people, each with their own story. Travel gives me a sense of freedom, curiosity and connection."

I kept noticing something though. The gap between how a place actually felt when you were standing in it, and how flat that feeling looked once it reached the online world. Somewhere between the photo and the post, the atmosphere got lost. That observation led to Beyond Your Stay.

We started working on location, translating what actually makes each place different instead of applying templates that work for anyone. A good story starts with paying attention. That's why BYS works across hospitality and leisure. These industries are built entirely around experience, and that kind of attention actually matters.

Yoran RuisFounder, Beyond Your Stay

Our compass

The bird that never stays.

The Arctic tern flies further than any other bird on earth, from pole to pole and back, every single year. It never really settles anywhere for long. It breeds on the Wadden coast, the same stretch of coastline where Beyond Your Stay took its first real steps.

Before it moves, it watches. It flies calm and high, reading the water, and only dives once it knows exactly where to go, one precise motion, rarely twice. That's the same order we work in: understand a place first, then act, never the other way around.

And it never stays in one place for good. Which is, in the end, the whole point of the name.
